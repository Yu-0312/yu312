## Ddk1dZkkwsn
Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6 is really not as simple as an ordinary "model upgrade" this time!
Why was MiMo-V2.6 able to improve so dramatically?

1️⃣ MixRL: truly scaling up RL
Capabilities such as coding, agents, vision, and safety are no longer trained completely separately, but are tackled together through mixed RL; complex, ultra-long, hard-to-verify tasks are trained on their own and then merged into the model's capabilities with MOPD.

2️⃣ Fully asynchronous GRPO at 1568×16
Per-step scale is pushed to 1568×16, with a cumulative 3.5B+ RL tokens and support for up to 1M context.

3️⃣ The reward mechanism is scaled too
GRS does within-group comparative scoring and GAR redistributes advantages, making the reward signal more effective.

4️⃣ Specifically targeting reward hacking
A frozen MoE router + multiple layers of protection address reward gaming, the problem that large-scale RL runs into most easily.

5️⃣ A comprehensive upgrade of the training recipe
Adam for pre-training, Muon + Row-Norm (Muown) for mid-training, combined with Hybrid SWA/GA + MTP.

📊 The model scale is also staggering:
Pro: 1.02T parameters / 42B active
Flash: 310B parameters / 15B active

The most noteworthy point: it's fully open source!!!

## Ddkdz_XGjmD
Around 5 p.m. yesterday I got word that Xiaomi was about to release a model, but compared with Claude or Codex it still has quite a few problems.
In the closed-beta version, mp3 and mp4 uploads are limited to 10 minutes at most. There are also some language issues: for example, if you set the language to Chinese, it will randomly switch to English in its output. It also blocks certain keywords — in my tests, topics related to sea freight and national policy were blocked.
As for strengths, I think it has an edge over other AIs at browsing Chinese media information, and for experimental modeling I had to adjust it fewer times than I did with Opus 4.8; as for how it compares with Astra, I'm not sure yet.
Finally, on cost-effectiveness, it really does beat well-known models like Grok 4.6 and GLM 5.3, so if you're a beginner who wants to use AI, or you call APIs often, I think it's worth a try!
